Daily POS Quick Reference & Counter Staff Workflow
Standard Operating Procedure
Easy 4-Step Daily Routine
1 Ring Up Counter Sale (Quick Sale / POS)
  1. Click Quick Sale or POS in the left navigation menu.
  2. Add items / services to invoice:
    • Type tire size (e.g. 205/55R16, 225/65R17) or service (Mount & Balance, Flat Repair).
    • For exhaust: select Muffler, Catalytic Converter, Pipe Welding, etc.
  3. Collect payment:
    • 💵 Cash: Enter amount received. Cash goes straight into your till drawer.
    • 💳 Card: Swipe or tap card on the counter payment terminal.
  4. Click Complete / Pay → Receipt prints or displays immediately!
2 Automatic Shop Separation

The POS automatically routes money into the right shop without any manual math:

Shop Keywords / Items Reports As
🛞 Tires Shop Tire sizes (205/55R16), Used Tires, New Tires, Mount, Balance, Valve Stem Tires Daily Gross, Tires Cash in Drawer
🔧 Mufflers Shop Muffler, Catalytic Converter, Exhaust Pipe, Hanger, Welding Labor Muffler Gross, Muffler Card/Cash Totals
💡 Multi-item sale? If a customer gets both a tire and a muffler weld, the POS automatically splits the revenue to each shop's ledger accurately.
3 Checking Money Received (Finance Page)
  • Open Finance from the left menu to view all transactions.
  • Cash Payments (💵):
    • Clearly tagged as 💵 Cash & ✓ In Cash Drawer.
    • No bank reference or payout confirmation needed — cash is already physically in your counter drawer!
  • Card Payments (💳):
    • Tagged as 💳 Card (Counter Terminal). Settled through card processor clearing.
✓ Zero Confusion: Cash is in hand immediately; card payments reconcile with your batch terminal report.
4 End of Day: Print Daily Sales PDF (< 1 Min)

At daily closing time, generate your printable sales report:

  1. Click Reports from the left menu.
  2. Date Filter is set to Today by default.
  3. Click your desired 1-click PDF button:
    • 🖨️ Print Tires PDF: Prints only Tires Shop sales & invoices.
    • 🖨️ Print Mufflers PDF: Prints only Mufflers Shop sales & invoices.
    • 🖨️ Print / Save as PDF: Complete consolidated shop report.
  4. Print directly to paper or choose "Save as PDF" for owner record keeping.
Daily Counter Staff Checklist
Morning Opening: Log into POS and confirm cash drawer starting float.
Every Customer: Create invoice in Quick Sale / POS, choose Cash or Card.
Evening Closing: Go to Reports → Click Print PDF → Match cash drawer.
