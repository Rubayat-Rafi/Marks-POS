Daily POS Quick Reference & Counter Staff Workflow
Standard Operating Procedure
Clean & Simple 4-Step Daily Routine
1 Ring Up Counter Sale (Quick Sale / POS)
  1. Click Quick Sale in the navigation menu.
  2. Add items / services to invoice:
    • Tires: type size (e.g. 205/55R16, 225/65R17) or service (Mount & Balance, Flat Repair).
    • Mufflers / Exhaust: select Muffler, Catalytic Converter, Flex Pipe, Welding Labor.
  3. Collect payment:
    • 💵 Cash: Enter amount tendered. Cash goes directly into register drawer.
    • 💳 Card: Swipe/tap on counter terminal.
  4. Click Complete / Pay → One-click Print Receipt or Download Invoice (PDF).
2 Automatic Shop Separation (Tires vs. Mufflers)

The POS automatically classifies items into their respective shops without any manual math:

Shop Division Sample Items Automated Tracking
🛞 Tires Shop Tires (205/55R16), Mount & Balance, Tire Repair, Valve Stems, Disposal Fee Tires Revenue, Tires Tax, Tires Invoices
🔧 Mufflers Shop Muffler, Catalytic Converter, Exhaust Pipe, Hangers, Custom Welding Labor Mufflers Revenue, Mufflers Tax, Mufflers Invoices
💡 Multi-department sale? If an invoice has both a tire and an exhaust weld, the system automatically tracks each shop's net total, tax, and sales breakdown.
3 Daily Sales & Invoices Screen

Open Daily Sales & Invoices from the menu for a clean overview:

  • Shop Selector Pill: Easily isolate shop data with one click:
    🏠 All Shops   🛞 Tires Shop   🔧 Mufflers Shop
  • Selecting Tires Shop isolates only Tires sales, totals, and invoices.
  • Selecting Mufflers Shop isolates only Mufflers sales, totals, and invoices.
  • Clean Summary Cards: Total Sales, Sales Before Tax, Sales Tax, and Shop Totals.
  • Date Presets: One click for Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, This Month, or Custom dates.
4 Simple Clean Reports & Invoices (< 1 Min)

Clean, straightforward reporting with zero unnecessary clutter:

  1. Daily Sales Report (Simple Header + Table + Total):
    • Click Print Daily Sales (PDF) at top of page.
    • Generates a clean header followed immediately by the invoice register table with TOTAL (no extra redundant summary cards).
  2. Individual Customer Invoices (Separate Design):
    • In the Invoices Table, every row has View Invoice, Print / PDF, and Delete.
    • Clicking Print / PDF generates an official, standalone Sales Invoice (with Bill-To box, itemized breakdown, tax, and signatures) formatted for 1 page.
✓ Clean 1-Page Layouts: Zero browser URLs or page-overflow clutter when printing or saving as PDF.
Daily Counter Staff Checklist
Morning Opening: Open POS / Quick Sale & verify cash drawer float.
Every Customer: Create sale in Quick Sale, collect Cash/Card, print/download receipt.
Evening Closing: Go to Daily Sales & Invoices → Click Print Daily Sales (PDF) → Balance drawer.
